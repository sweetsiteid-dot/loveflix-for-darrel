Episode 01: I'm Glad It's You 🤍

sayang, gua seneng banget bisa sampai di titik ini sama lu, el. thank you for being part of my life, for making me smile in your own little ways, and for choosing me.

gua emang nggak jago ngerangkai kata romantis, gengsi gua aja udah setinggi burj khalifa WKWKWK, tapi rasa sayang gua ke lu melebihi itu. makanya gua lebih sering nunjukin rasa sayang gua lewat hal-hal kecil, kayak chibi yang gua bikin buat lu, manips yg gua bikin, channel yg gua bikin.

gua harap kita bisa terus belajar buat ngerti satu sama lain, tumbuh bareng, dan sama-sama memperbaiki apa pun yang perlu diperbaiki. i don't need us to be perfect, i just want us to keep trying and choosing each other.

gua sayang banget sama lu, el, walaupun lu kadang ngeselin dan bikin gua pengen nonjok BUT STILL, i'm really glad it's you.

semoga cerita kita masih panjang ya el.

i love you, my favorite headache. 👻🤍
